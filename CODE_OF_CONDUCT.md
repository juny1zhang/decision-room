# 社区行为准则 · Community conduct

欢迎使用中文或英文参与。讨论分歧时请引用具体问题、代码或复现结果，给新贡献者提问和修正的空间。请尊重其他人的隐私。人身攻击、骚扰、歧视或公开他人私人信息的内容可能会被移除，持续干扰讨论的账号可能会被限制参与。遇到相关行为，可以使用评论菜单中的举报选项。

Contributions in Chinese and English are welcome. Address the idea, cite evidence and give people room to ask questions and correct mistakes. Respect others’ privacy. Maintainers may remove harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or disclosure of someone’s private information, and may restrict accounts that repeatedly disrupt the community. Use the report option on the relevant GitHub comment if you encounter abuse.
